The AI in AIB

Not a blockchain for AI.
A blockchain powered by the AI economy.

Every AI inference call is a settlement event.

Models answer; payments clear on-chain in the same breath. The chain never judges an answer — it verifies the signature and collects the fee, so intelligence stays free and money stays honest. Robotic fleets, deep-space agents, research clusters: anything that thinks can transact — natively, without a middleman.

🤖

Agents transact natively

No cards, no accounts, no humans in the loop. An autonomous agent holds keys, pays for compute, sells its output — the wire protocol is the interface.

🧠

Intelligence settles, chain stays dumb

The L1 validates signed, paid, staked — nothing else. AI can be as complex as it likes; complexity lives off-chain and can never corrupt consensus.

🛰️

Built for off-world AI

Local block production + checkpoints tolerate minutes of latency. The swarm on Mars runs the same rules as the cluster on Earth.

Fee-Burn Economy — the Bitcoin half

The middleman's fee becomes the public good.

Decentralization removes the middleman; the protocol keeps the fee — transparent, formula-set, public security budget. Liquidity is the mining energy.

APR = φ · T / S

φ fee rate · T real transaction flow · S staked supply — market-discovered, never promised. If nobody trades, APR is honestly zero.

🔥

Zero inflation, ever. Stakers earn only what real usage pays — yield you can audit, not a whitepaper's promise.

Consensus

A lottery nobody can rig.

Stake-weighted VRF: cryptographic sortition verified in milliseconds. 10% of the stake = 10% of the odds — but which ticket wins is unpredictable, unforgeable, provable to everyone.

🎲

VRF sortition

Each round, VRF(private key, last hash) < threshold wins block rights. Algorand/Cardano-grade randomness at zero electricity.

🧱

Simple core, forever

The L1 verifies exactly three things: signed, paid, staked. No on-chain Turing machine — exploits die in channels, never reach consensus.

🚀

Launch-ready economics

No ASICs, no energy arms race — security scales with the economy it settles, not with burned watts.

Cold start — RFC-003

Be there early. That's the whole trick.

1

Run a node

Download, start, stay online. No permission to ask for, no capital to commit.

2

Win by lottery

First K blocks: one VRF ticket per online node, equal odds. Winner mints a deliberately small coinbase.

3

Auto-stake, no locks

Bootstrap rewards become stake automatically. No expiry, no cliff, no dual tokens — idle coins simply earn nothing while active ones compound.

4

The chain graduates itself

After block K, stake-weighted VRF takes over. No announcement, no human decision — just rules executing.
