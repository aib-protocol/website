Roadmap

Our story starting from 2015

2015

Original AIB chain

Advanced Integrated Blockchains launched as a Bitcoin-fork (scrypt + AuxPoW merge mining). The original chain and its exchanges are long gone — we keep this entry only as history.

2026

Reborn: Fee-Burn Economy

AIB is rebuilt from scratch in Go as a pure economic-validation chain: no premine, no founder grant, no admin keys. Staking APR = φ·T/S — set by real transaction flow, never by promise. (RFC-002)

  • Source

2026

Bootstrap Window

The first K blocks are minted by presence, not by stake: pure VRF lottery, one ticket per online node, zero permission. Then the chain switches itself to stake-weighted VRF — automatically. (RFC-003)

  • Discussion

Next

Ecosystem: L2 & payments

High-speed trading L2, Lightning-style payment channels, trustless BTC/ETH/USDT anchoring, and native embeddability for robots and deep-space agents. The L1 stays minimal forever. (RFC-004)

About Us

Decentralization removes the middleman — and the AIB protocol collects the middleman’s fee as public security budget. Every trade pays a fee (φ); fees flow to stakers; stakers secure the network. The chain itself does exactly three things: verify signatures, collect fees, and weigh stake. Everything complex — smart contracts, AI agents, DeFi — lives outside the chain and settles onto it. An exploit destroys one channel; it can never reach consensus. AIB aims to be as simple as Bitcoin, as flexible as the ecosystem built on top of it, and durable enough to run on another planet.
